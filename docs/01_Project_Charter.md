# Document 01: Project Charter
## Project: Nursing Administration Portal
**Client:** Jazan Specialty Hospital (JSH)
**Date:** July 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Vision
To establish a state-of-the-art, secure, and user-centric digital portal that empowers the Nursing Administration at Jazan Specialty Hospital to achieve operational excellence, ensure seamless communication, and maintain the highest standards of healthcare quality and patient safety in alignment with Saudi Vision 2030.

## 2. Mission
To digitize and centralize all nursing governance, operational schedules, policies, educational resources, and quality metrics into a single, accessible platform, thereby reducing administrative burden and enabling nursing staff to focus more on direct patient care.

## 3. Objectives
*   **Centralization:** Consolidate 100% of nursing policies, procedures, and forms into a single, searchable digital library within 3 months of launch.
*   **Efficiency:** Reduce the time spent on administrative scheduling and document retrieval by 40%.
*   **Compliance:** Achieve 100% compliance with CBAHI and JCI documentation accessibility standards.
*   **Engagement:** Increase nursing staff engagement and satisfaction by providing transparent access to news, training, and recognition programs.
*   **Security:** Ensure strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to protect sensitive administrative data.

## 4. Scope
*   **In Scope:**
    *   Development of a responsive web application (Portal) accessible via hospital intranet and secure external links.
    *   Content Management System (CMS) for News, Announcements, and Events.
    *   Interactive Organizational Structure and Departmental pages.
    *   Governance module for Councils and Committees (minutes, decisions, tracking).
    *   Document Control Center (Policies, Procedures, Forms, Guidelines) with versioning.
    *   Dashboards for Scheduling, Quality Metrics, and Training.
    *   User Management with granular Roles and Permissions (RBAC).
    *   Global Search functionality.
*   **Out of Scope:**
    *   Integration with Electronic Medical Records (EMR) for direct patient clinical documentation.
    *   Direct financial, payroll, or billing processing.
    *   Hospital Information System (HIS) core modules (e.g., pharmacy dispensing, lab results).

## 5. Success Criteria
1.  **Adoption Rate:** 80% of nursing staff log into the portal at least once a week within the first 6 months.
2.  **System Performance:** Average page load time is under 2 seconds.
3.  **Audit Success:** Zero non-conformities related to document accessibility during the next CBAHI/JCI audit.
4.  **User Satisfaction:** Achieve a User Satisfaction Score (CSAT) of > 85% in post-launch surveys.

## 6. Stakeholders
*   **Project Sponsor:** Director of Nursing (DON).
*   **Business Owners:** Heads of Nursing Quality, Education, and Operations.
*   **End Users:** Head Nurses, Clinical Resource Nurses (CRN), Registered Nurses.
*   **Technical Partners:** JSH IT Department, External Software Development Agency, Cybersecurity Team.

## 7. Timeline (High-Level SDLC)
*   **Month 1:** Business Discovery & Requirements Engineering (Phases 1-2)
*   **Month 2:** Information Architecture & UX/UI Design (Phases 3-4)
*   **Month 3:** System Design & Initial Development (Phases 5-6)
*   **Month 4:** Core Development & Integration
*   **Month 5:** Testing, UAT, and Security Audits
*   **Month 6:** Training, Migration, and Go-Live (Phase 7)

## 8. Assumptions
*   The hospital IT infrastructure supports modern web technologies and cloud/containerized hosting.
*   Active Directory / SSO (Single Sign-On) APIs will be made available by the hospital IT team if requested.
*   Stakeholders will be available for timely reviews and approvals of phase deliverables.

## 9. Constraints
*   **Regulatory:** Must comply strictly with Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) guidelines and Ministry of Health (MOH) data privacy laws.
*   **Time:** Must be ready for the upcoming accreditation cycle.
*   **Budget:** Fixed budget allocated for the digital transformation initiative.
