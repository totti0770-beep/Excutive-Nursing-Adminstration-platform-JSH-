# Document 12: Content Strategy
## Project: Nursing Administration Portal
**Client:** Jazan Specialty Hospital (JSH)
**Phase:** 3 - Information Architecture
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Overview (نظرة عامة)
This document defines how content will be created, managed, and maintained within the portal to ensure it remains accurate, engaging, and aligned with hospital standards.

## 2. Content Types (أنواع المحتوى)
*   **Static Content:** Terms of Reference, organizational charts. Updated rarely.
*   **Dynamic Content:** News, announcements, urgent alerts. Updated frequently.
*   **Controlled Documents:** Clinical policies, procedures, standard forms. Subject to strict versioning and approval workflows.
*   **Data-driven Content:** Schedules, KPI dashboards, recognition leaderboards. Uploaded or synced periodically.

## 3. Content Ownership & Governance (ملكية المحتوى وحوكمته)
*   **Global News:** Managed by DON office and Portal Managers.
*   **Policies:** Owned by the Nursing Quality Department.
*   **Department Schedules:** Owned by respective Department Heads.
*   **Training Events:** Owned by the Nursing Education Department.

## 4. Tone and Voice (نبرة الصوت)
*   **Professional & Clinical:** All official policies and procedures must use formal, standardized medical terminology.
*   **Empowering & Engaging:** News, announcements, and recognition posts should be motivational, fostering a positive workplace culture.
*   **Clear & Concise:** Urgent alerts must be direct, removing ambiguity to ensure immediate understanding.

## 5. Content Lifecycle (دورة حياة المحتوى)
1.  **Draft:** Content is created but not visible to standard users.
2.  **Review/Approve:** Content is reviewed by designated authorities (e.g., Quality Officer for policies).
3.  **Publish:** Content becomes visible to the target audience.
4.  **Archive:** Content is automatically hidden upon expiration (e.g., an expired policy or an old event).
