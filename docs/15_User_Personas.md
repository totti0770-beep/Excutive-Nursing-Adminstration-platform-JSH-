# Document 15: User Personas
## Project: Nursing Administration Portal
**Client:** Jazan Specialty Hospital (JSH)
**Phase:** 4 - UX/UI
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Overview (نظرة عامة)
User personas represent the primary target audiences of the portal. They help the design and development teams understand user needs, goals, and frustrations, ensuring the system is built with empathy and usability in mind.

## 2. Persona 1: The Frontline Staff Nurse
*   **Name:** Fatima (فاطمة)
*   **Role:** Registered Nurse, ER Department
*   **Age:** 28
*   **Tech Savviness:** High (Heavy smartphone user)
*   **Goals:** Wants quick access to her monthly shifts. Needs to find clinical policies (like medication administration) instantly during a busy shift without leaving the ward.
*   **Frustrations:** Dislikes scrolling through hundreds of WhatsApp messages to find an old announcement. Hates paper binders that are often missing pages.
*   **Needs from Portal:** Mobile-first design, fast search bar, Dark Mode (for night shifts).

## 3. Persona 2: The Department Head
*   **Name:** Ahmed (أحمد)
*   **Role:** Head Nurse, ICU
*   **Age:** 42
*   **Tech Savviness:** Medium
*   **Goals:** Needs to easily upload the monthly roster, nominate his best nurses for awards, and track his department's compliance with quality metrics.
*   **Frustrations:** Spends too much time doing administrative paperwork. Struggles to ensure all his staff have read the latest policy updates.
*   **Needs from Portal:** Easy upload interface for schedules, a dashboard showing who hasn't acknowledged critical alerts, simple nomination forms.

## 4. Persona 3: The Quality Officer
*   **Name:** Dr. Sarah (د. سارة)
*   **Role:** Nursing Quality & Patient Safety Officer
*   **Age:** 35
*   **Tech Savviness:** High
*   **Goals:** Ensure the hospital is 100% ready for CBAHI/JCI audits. Wants strict version control over all policies and a clear archive of expired documents.
*   **Frustrations:** Finding outdated printed policies on the floor. Managing policy approvals through slow email chains.
*   **Needs from Portal:** Desktop-optimized interface for uploading PDFs, metadata tagging, clear version history, and expiry date alerts.

## 5. Persona 4: The Executive (DON)
*   **Name:** Dr. Khalid (د. خالد)
*   **Role:** Director of Nursing
*   **Age:** 50
*   **Tech Savviness:** Medium
*   **Goals:** Monitor the overall performance of the nursing administration. Ensure seamless top-down communication.
*   **Frustrations:** Lack of centralized data. Cannot easily see hospital-wide statistics without requesting reports.
*   **Needs from Portal:** Executive dashboard with high-level KPIs, ability to publish hospital-wide news, and secure access to executive council minutes.
