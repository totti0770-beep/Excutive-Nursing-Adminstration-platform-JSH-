# Document 03: Stakeholder Analysis
## Project: Nursing Administration Portal
**Client:** Jazan Specialty Hospital (JSH)
**Phase:** 1 - Business Discovery
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Introduction
This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the Nursing Administration Portal project, analyzing their roles, needs, expectations, and their level of influence and priority. Understanding these stakeholders is critical to ensuring the system meets business objectives and achieves high adoption rates.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Profiles

### 2.1 مدير التمريض (Director of Nursing - DON)
*   **Responsibilities:** Ultimate accountability for nursing operations, quality of care, strategic planning, and portal sponsorship.
*   **Needs:** High-level dashboards for hospital-wide nursing metrics, tools for official communication, and oversight of governance councils.
*   **Expectations:** A secure, executive-grade platform that aligns with Saudi Vision 2030, reduces administrative overhead, and ensures audit readiness.
*   **Influence:** High
*   **Priority:** High

### 2.2 المساعد (Assistant Director of Nursing - ADON)
*   **Responsibilities:** Daily operational oversight, managing department heads, and acting as a bridge between the DON and the frontline.
*   **Needs:** Quick access to departmental schedules, staffing reports, and the ability to review and approve policies or announcements.
*   **Expectations:** Streamlined workflows, reduction in paper-based reporting, and real-time alerts for critical operational issues.
*   **Influence:** High
*   **Priority:** High

### 2.3 رؤساء الأقسام (Department Heads / Head Nurses)
*   **Responsibilities:** Managing ward operations, staffing rosters, evaluating nurses, and ensuring departmental compliance with policies.
*   **Needs:** Tools to upload/manage monthly schedules, nominate staff for recognition, and view department-specific quality metrics.
*   **Expectations:** A user-friendly interface that saves them time compared to current Excel/paper methods, and easy access to HR/Admin forms.
*   **Influence:** High
*   **Priority:** High

### 2.4 قسم الجودة (Nursing Quality & Patient Safety)
*   **Responsibilities:** Maintaining accreditation standards (CBAHI/JCI), document control, policy updates, and tracking quality indicators.
*   **Needs:** A robust CMS for version-controlled policies, audit logs, and dashboards to track compliance and patient safety metrics.
*   **Expectations:** Strict versioning control, clear approval workflows, and assurance that old policies are archived securely.
*   **Influence:** High
*   **Priority:** High

### 2.5 قسم التعليم (Nursing Education & Training)
*   **Responsibilities:** Organizing Continuous Medical Education (CME), onboarding new staff, and tracking training compliance.
*   **Needs:** A calendar for training events, tools to publish educational resources, and tracking capabilities for staff attendance/certifications.
*   **Expectations:** Easy dissemination of training materials and integration of multimedia (videos, presentations) within the portal.
*   **Influence:** Medium
*   **Priority:** Medium

### 2.6 الموظفون (Staff Nurses / End Users)
*   **Responsibilities:** Providing direct patient care and adhering to hospital policies and schedules.
*   **Needs:** Mobile-friendly, instant access to their schedules, clinical policies, quick forms, and hospital news.
*   **Expectations:** A fast, intuitive app-like experience (preferably Dark Mode for night shifts) that doesn't require complex training to use.
*   **Influence:** Medium
*   **Priority:** High (They determine the adoption success criteria)

### 2.7 قسم تقنية المعلومات (IT Department)
*   **Responsibilities:** Infrastructure provisioning, cybersecurity compliance, network stability, and potentially active directory integration.
*   **Needs:** Clear technical architecture documents, security clearance, deployment scripts, and API specifications.
*   **Expectations:** A secure, scalable solution that complies with National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) guidelines without overwhelming their current support queue.
*   **Influence:** High
*   **Priority:** Medium

### 2.8 الإدارة العليا (Top Management / Hospital CEO)
*   **Responsibilities:** Overall hospital performance, budget allocation, and strategic direction.
*   **Needs:** Periodic reports on digital transformation progress and ROI (Return on Investment) regarding administrative efficiency.
*   **Expectations:** A successful project delivery on time and within budget that enhances the hospital's reputation and accreditation standing.
*   **Influence:** High
*   **Priority:** Low (For day-to-day project execution, but high for final sign-off)
