# Document 10: Site Map
## Project: Nursing Administration Portal
**Client:** Jazan Specialty Hospital (JSH)
**Phase:** 3 - Information Architecture
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Overview (نظرة عامة)
The Site Map outlines the hierarchical structure and navigation paths of the Nursing Administration Portal. It ensures a logical flow of information, enabling users to find policies, schedules, and news intuitively.

## 2. Site Structure (هيكلية الموقع)

*   **1.0 Login / Authentication (تسجيل الدخول)**
    *   1.1 Sign In
    *   1.2 Forgot Password

*   **2.0 Dashboard / Home (الرئيسية)**
    *   2.1 Key Metrics (Visibility depends on Role)
    *   2.2 Urgent Alerts Banner
    *   2.3 News Feed & Announcements
    *   2.4 Quick Links (Favorite Policies/Forms)

*   **3.0 Departments (الأقسام)**
    *   3.1 Department Directory
    *   3.2 Department Detail Page (e.g., ICU, ER, OPD)
        *   3.2.1 Department Structure & Leadership
        *   3.2.2 Staff Rosters & Schedules
        *   3.2.3 Departmental KPIs

*   **4.0 Document Control & Quality (الجودة والسياسات)**
    *   4.1 Search Hub (Global Search)
    *   4.2 Clinical Policies
    *   4.3 Administrative Policies
    *   4.4 Standard Forms & Templates
    *   4.5 Clinical Guidelines

*   **5.0 Governance & Councils (الحوكمة واللجان)**
    *   5.1 Nursing Executive Council
    *   5.2 Nursing Quality Council
    *   5.3 Nursing Education Council
    *   5.4 [Council Detail Page]
        *   5.4.1 Terms of Reference (ToR)
        *   5.4.2 Member Roster
        *   5.4.3 Meeting Agendas & Minutes (Secure access)

*   **6.0 Education & Training (التعليم والتدريب)**
    *   6.1 Training Calendar (CME Events)
    *   6.2 Educational Resources & Presentations
    *   6.3 Onboarding Materials

*   **7.0 Recognition (التقدير)**
    *   7.1 Wall of Fame (Top Nurses, Top Heads)
    *   7.2 Submit a Nomination (Role-restricted)

*   **8.0 Admin Panel (لوحة التحكم) - *Role Restricted***
    *   8.1 User Management (Add, Edit, Deactivate)
    *   8.2 Role & Permissions Configuration
    *   8.3 CMS (News & Announcements Manager)
    *   8.4 System Logs & Audit Trail

*   **9.0 User Profile (الملف الشخصي)**
    *   9.1 View Profile Details
    *   9.2 Change Password
    *   9.3 Notification Preferences
    *   9.4 Logout
