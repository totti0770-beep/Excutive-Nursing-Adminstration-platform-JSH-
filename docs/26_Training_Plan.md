# Document 26: Training Plan
## Project: Nursing Administration Portal
**Client:** Jazan Specialty Hospital (JSH)
**Phase:** 7 - Training & Launch
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Overview (نظرة عامة)
To ensure high adoption rates and minimal disruption to daily operations, a structured training plan is essential. This document outlines how different user groups will be educated on using the Nursing Administration Portal.

## 2. Train-the-Trainer Model (نموذج تدريب المدربين)
Instead of training all 1,000+ nurses directly, the project will employ a Train-the-Trainer approach.
*   **Super Users:** Identify 5-10 tech-savvy individuals (e.g., Clinical Resource Nurses or Quality Link Nurses) across different shifts and departments.
*   **Training:** These Super Users will receive intensive, hands-on training for the portal.
*   **Role:** They will act as the first point of contact for frontline staff on their wards, answering basic questions and demonstrating features.

## 3. Targeted Training Sessions (الجلسات التدريبية المخصصة)

### 3.1 Admin & Content Managers (DON, Quality, Education)
*   **Duration:** 4-hour workshop.
*   **Format:** Hands-on lab session in the hospital IT training room.
*   **Curriculum:** Managing users/roles, uploading policies, metadata tagging, using the CMS for news, archiving documents.

### 3.2 Department Heads (رؤساء الأقسام)
*   **Duration:** 2-hour session.
*   **Format:** Virtual meeting (Teams/Zoom) or in-person session.
*   **Curriculum:** Accessing department dashboards, uploading monthly schedules, submitting recognition nominations.

## 4. End-User Training Materials (مواد تدريب المستخدمين)
For standard nurses, classroom training is impractical. Instead, self-service materials will be provided:
*   **Video Tutorials:** Short, 1-2 minute screencasts (with Arabic voiceovers) demonstrating:
    *   How to log in and reset passwords.
    *   How to find and read your monthly schedule.
    *   How to search for a specific policy.
*   **Quick Reference Guides (QRGs):** 1-page PDF/infographics that can be printed and posted on ward notice boards.
*   **In-App Guidance:** A first-login tutorial overlay highlighting key features (Search Bar, Notifications).
